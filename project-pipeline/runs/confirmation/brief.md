# Brief: Confirmation — probation to confirmed, without the spreadsheet
- **Problem / idea:** HR tracks probation end dates in spreadsheets, chases managers for feedback over email, and confirmations slip past their due date. The confirmation letter, the status change and the follow-up tasks are all manual. Confirmation tracks every probation, starts the review on time, routes it through reviewers with a structured evaluation, and on the final decision updates the employee record, issues the letter and starts the follow-up tasks automatically.
- **Target users:** HR admins (set the policy, track the pipeline), reporting managers (evaluate and recommend), the HR head as final reviewer (decide), and employees (see the outcome and acknowledge the letter).
- **Platform:** Web (1440×900)
- **Audience for the video:** Recruiters and hiring managers viewing Prithvi's product portfolio. They want the product thinking, not a sales pitch.
- **Video length:** 40 seconds target (not produced in this run).
- **What the mockup is:** A mockup of the **hero flow only**: one screen per flow step, default state, wired so the flow clicks through. **Not a working model.**
- **Tone:** Confident and plain-spoken. Short sentences.
- **Visual style / brand:** Match the portfolio site and `runs/letters/03-mockup/index.html` (lavender and mustard on white, Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree, 20px cards, pill buttons).
- **Must include:**
  1. Policy: probation by employee category, reviewer levels, start N days before due, auto-forward, extension cap, evaluation form.
  2. Probation tracker: confirmations due, auto-initiated, overdue and extension counts.
  3. Manager review: evaluation, recommendation (confirm / extend / do not confirm), feedback for the employee vs. private remarks.
  4. Final reviewer decision with the previous level's recommendation and a timeline.
  5. On confirmation: status change, letter issued for signature, eligibility updates and follow-up tasks, all automatic.
  6. Employee sees the outcome, the manager's feedback, and acknowledges the letter.
- **Out of scope:** Extension and termination branches as separate screens, settings pages, mobile app, empty/error/loading states, real company names or greytHR branding.
- **Data rule:** All names are fictional (NDA). Product name: "Confirmation". Company: "Northwind Components Pvt Ltd".
