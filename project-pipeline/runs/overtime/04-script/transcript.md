## Full narration

Factory overtime lives in spreadsheets and chat. Hours get missed, and payroll fixes them by hand. Overtime serves three people. Asha on the line, Vikram her manager, Priya in payroll. One plain policy sets the rules. Five hours twenty clocked becomes four paid hours. Asha never types hours. Short days are blocked. She ticks two days, and the cap meter updates. She adds a note and submits. Both requests go to Vikram. She can withdraw until it's decided. Now, over to Vikram. Hours arrive policy-checked, with the amount and reason. Vikram approves in one click. Then on to payroll. Priya picks the payroll month. September hours become an arrear on their own. The register shows regular lines, the arrear, and a reversal for hours cut after payout. Many workers never log in, so Vikram applies for them. Joseph's last day would cross his monthly cap, so that row is pink and locked. The rest are approved at once. Every rupee is checked, approved once, and paid in the right month. I took it from zero to one, to over a hundred enterprises. Extra time in attendance, paid by the rules.
