- [2] https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3479/2022/08/Houseplant-Watering-Recommendations.pdf — UConn Extension: waterlogged mix displaces oxygen, roots die and root rot sets in; check soil before watering.
- [3] https://www.gardeningetc.com/news/reasons-why-your-houseplant-is-dying — Gardening Etc: overwatering named as the top reason houseplants die; plants "killed with kindness".
