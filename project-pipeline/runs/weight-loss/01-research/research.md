# Research: Weight Loss Tracker
> No research stage for this run: it is Prithvi's own shipped personal project. This stub records the opportunities the product was built around, all marked as assumptions from Prithvi's own experience.

## Problem summary
Calorie trackers ask for too much typing per meal, so logging stops and the data stops being useful.

## Users & pain points
- **Alex, busy adult losing weight:** wants to know "am I under budget today?" without searching food databases for every ingredient.

## Current alternatives
Not researched for this run.

## Constraints
- Phone-first; must work as an installable app and on a weak connection.
- AI reads photos imperfectly, so people must be able to correct it.

## UX patterns worth reusing
- Photo-first capture with a confirm step. A daily budget ring or bar. Trend charts over week/month/year.

## Opportunities
- **O1** Take the typing out of food logging with photo recognition (assumption).
- **O2** Keep trust by treating every AI result as an editable draft (assumption).
- **O3** Make the daily target meaningful by deriving it from BMR, and show eaten vs burned vs target (assumption).
- **O4** Log weight from the scale display in one snap (assumption).
- **O5** Show cause and effect: weight trend next to calories in and out (assumption).
