## The problem

Accounts payable teams spent hours keying in invoice data and deciding who should handle each invoice.

## What I did

- Led the generative AI work for source-to-pay invoice data extraction, managing a team of APMs.
- Automated invoice routing using historical patterns and invoice coding.
- Built the business case for large-scale model training and launched Invoice Predictive Path.

## Outcome

- Extraction accuracy up 15–20%.
- Manual assignment time down 30%, freeing a week of AP team time every month.
- Invoice processing time down 12% and earned discounts up 6%.
