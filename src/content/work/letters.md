## The problem

HR writes offer, address-proof, experience and increment letters by editing Word files by hand. Signatures are chased over email and PDFs go out one at a time. That means typos in salaries, inconsistent formats across locations, no audit trail, and employees waiting on HR for a simple letter.

## Who it's for

- **HR admins** issue letters quickly, often in bulk during an increment cycle.
- **Authorised signatories**, such as the head of HR, sign with confidence from one queue.
- **Employees** receive, keep and acknowledge their letters in one place.

## Key decisions

- **One template, many letters.** Merge fields fill each employee's data, and a number series gives every letter a reference, so a batch of 24 is as easy as one.
- **Approval is a choice per run.** Address proofs can go straight out. Increment letters wait for the signatory.
- **Signing happens in the signatory's inbox,** with a preview and a timeline, instead of a PDF bounced over email.
- **Release is its own step.** Approval says the letter is right. Release decides how it reaches people: portal, email, password-protected PDF, and whether it must be acknowledged.
