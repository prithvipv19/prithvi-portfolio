## What it is

A no-code AI agent builder inside o9's enterprise planning platform. Teams describe the work they want an agent to do and deploy it themselves, without waiting on engineering.

## My role

I led the product work on the builder's UI, from requirements to release, working across platform, AI and design teams.

## Outcome

- Teams created and deployed 35+ agents in 3 months.
- Product-team effort dropped by nearly 40%.
- Our own agents for PRDs, test cases and bug analysis cut team effort by about 30% and sped up bug triage about 6x.
